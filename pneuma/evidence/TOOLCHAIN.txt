The commission's spine — 48,000 millet grains as simulated rigid bodies, two runs
one trajectory hash, 10,800 frames at 3840×2160 rendered from simulation state —
**cannot be executed on this host.** Not "will be slow": cannot run. A claim
otherwise would be a false beat under §32, and F34 is the failure this agency
already owns.
