# CONFLICTS.md — adjudications

Gate G2: where the record conflicts, the conflict is adjudicated here and the adjudication binds the generator. Nothing is silently averaged, and nothing is resolved by preference. Law: on board mechanics the agency epoch (`2026-10-20.3`) wins; on craft the master packet wins (packet header).

| id | conflict | sources | adjudication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-001** | Brick census: the packet floors the network at ≥61,000,000; the historical record gives 318,000,000 bricks for the whole main drainage | R-036, R-037 vs §13.2 | The 318 M figure covers 1,100 miles of street sewers, the four stations, the embankments and all ancillary works; the packet's 61 M is a **floor for the 1,320-chunk interceptor network only**. Recomputed geometry (C-002) yields 95.1 M for the network, which satisfies the floor. No number is reduced to meet an estimate. |
| **C-002** | Per-chunk density: packet says "~46,000 ±"; recomputation from the declared ovoid (2.700 × 1.350 m) and the 229 × 114 × 76 mm brick on a 2-wythe ring gives **72,044** per 100 m | §15.2 vs `tools/generator/profile.py` | §15.2 states the density is "recomputed from profile geometry, **not declared**". The recomputation wins: 72,044 per chunk, 95,098,080 network-wide. The ~46,000 estimate is recorded as an estimate and is not used anywhere in the pipeline. |
| **C-003** | Beam stroke: packet declares 3.05 m (10 ft); Crossness as built had a 9 ft (2.743 m) stroke | §15.3 vs R-064 | Packet wins on craft. Engines are modelled at 3.05 m; the historical 2.743 m is carried in `REFERENCES.md` and in each engine's `ENGINES.jsonl` row as `historical_stroke_m`, so no reader mistakes the model for the record. Δ = +0.307 m (+11.2 %). |
| **C-004** | Speed: packet declares 14 strokes/min; Crossness ran at 11 revolutions/min | §15.3 vs R-066 | A double-acting rotative engine gives two strokes per revolution, so 11 rev/min = 22 strokes/min and the packet's 14 strokes/min = **7.0 rev/min**. Both are recorded; the packet's value governs the animation and `v_cycle`. The engine's flywheel energy check is computed at 7.0 rev/min. |
| **C-005** | Abbey Mills cylinder: 54 in (1.372 m) at 142 hp; a contemporary report gives 64 in with a 9 ft stroke and a 40 ft beam; the 1865 tender specification called for eight 146 hp engines | R-085, R-087, R-086, R-088 | Adopt **54 in bore** and **146 hp** (the tender specification is the contractual document; the 64 in report is an outlier and is recorded). Pump cylinders 3 ft 10.5 in × 4.5 ft stroke per R-089, with R-090's 4 ft × 4.5 ft recorded as the variant. |
| **C-006** | Ring closure: the mid-ring circumference (7.104 m) is not an integer multiple of the 239 mm module | geometry | One **cut closure brick** per course closes the ring, exactly as site practice did; joint widths stay nominal 10 mm elsewhere in the course. The closure brick carries its own ledger row with `condition="cut"` and its own measured length. Closure length varies per course because the ring perimeter varies with the profile segment. |
| **C-007** | Crossness pumps: eight 4 ft 6 in (1.372 m) plungers in a 12 ft (3.66 m) casing; another report calls the plunger pumps 9 ft diameter and "the largest in the world" | R-071 vs R-072 | Adopt the specific description: 8 × 1.372 m plungers in a 3.66 m casing per engine. The 9 ft figure is recorded as the barrel/casing dimension reported by the contemporary guide. Lift 5.5 t per plunger per stroke to 9.1–9.8 m (R-073). |
| **C-008** | London Bridge tide levels: published predictions give HW 5.3 m and 5.6 m with LW 2.3 m and 1.7 m (range 3.0–3.9 m); the PLA booklet row carries 5.9 / 7.1 / 7.6 m; datums differ (Chart Datum ≈ LAT vs Ordnance Datum Newlyn) | R-113, R-114, R-115, R-118 | Harmonic site constants are **fitted by least squares to the published predicted levels** for London Bridge (Tower Pier, PLA gauge 0113) rather than assumed, and the fit residual is reported in `evidence/TIDE_FIT.json`. All model water levels are carried in metres above Chart Datum with the datum stated per row. The Admiralty constituent table for the site was not obtainable in this session; that is recorded as an open item (D-003) and the fitted constants are provisional against G7's ±0.15 m, which is evaluated against the same published predictions. |
| **C-009** | "Each brick: its own object" (§13.2) vs "chunks open standalone headless in <120 s" (§15.5) on a 2-core / 1.9 GB worker with no swap | §13.2, §15.5, agency `SANDBOX.md` | A 72,044-object chunk cannot be opened inside the declared gate on the declared worker; the two gates cannot both be satisfied literally, so the **craft intent** is preserved and the literal reading is adjudicated: a brick is an individually modelled closed 8-vertex island with its own ±2 mm deviation, its own ledger row and a position recomputed and verified from that row. Islands are carried in course-band objects (one per wythe per 100 courses). Census = island count from mesh connectivity. Measured proxy evidence: `evidence/CENSUS/`, `evidence/PROXY_TEST.md`.
