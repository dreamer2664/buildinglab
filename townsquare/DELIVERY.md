## Open and edit

Unzip Master and open `Master/Winterlichtplatz.blend` in Blender4.3.2 or a compatible version. Maps are packed; no add-on/network is needed to open the model. Named building/floor/room collections and92camera presets are editable. In the Text Editor run embedded `VIEW_TOOLS.py`, then use `apply_view('B01_L01_interior')`, `apply_view('B01_L01_plan')`, `apply_view('B01_L01_cutaway')` or `apply_view('H02')`. Separate editable human rigs accompany the static posed people in the town. Use the full native on a desktop with adequate RAM; the derivative is budgeted for this2GBworker.

The portable archive includes whole-town GLB; building archives provide separate assets. GLB cannot preserve Blender HDRI/area-light illumination or every procedural/glass shader. Portable photographic maps are capped512px; packed native keeps originals. Actual public clean-import tests and three neutral-light original proofs were inspected, not merely export logs.
