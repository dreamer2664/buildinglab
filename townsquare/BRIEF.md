# Winterlichtplatz — new Christmas town-square commission

Confirmed by the human: large square with 10–12 buildings; fully furnish EVERY occupied floor; all interiors different; Central European Christmas-market atmosphere; suitably licensed realistic people and original fictional business brands/logos. Supermarket, benches, trees, playground, lamps, surrounding buildings, Christmas decorations, convincing people and realistic lighting are mandatory. The previous residence remains unchanged and in independent review.

## Adopted original design
12 independent surrounding buildings; 26 occupied floors. A 64 × 52 m public square, steep roofs, weathered plaster/mineral masonry, slate/clay roofs, metalwork and timber. Neighborhood supermarket plus eleven complementary businesses. Full upper-floor homes, ateliers, reading rooms, consulting rooms and hotel suites, not empty windows. Original logos must appear consistently on fascia, projecting signs, glazing, bags, menus and appropriate interior graphics. Names are fictional project identities, not trademark-clearance assurances.

Blue-hour Christmas setting: tactile damp cobbles, accumulated snow on roofs/edges and planting rather than blanket white paint, warm practical storefronts, restrained warm-white festive lights, real branches/foliage and winter clothing. Christmas tree, eight small stalls, 24 benches, 24 street lamps, 12 deciduous trees, 36 planned people from at least12 close-view variants. Playground with credible fall zones, fencing/access and non-slip surfaces. These are design targets, not completed inventory.

## Realism first — changed bar from previous project
The previous stylised vegetation, constant-colour portable materials and simple figures must not be passed off as this project's final realism. Prove one ground-level shopfront/interior/material/lighting slice AND one realistically skinned, clothed and posed human at close range before repeating assets. No generated illustration, crowd billboard, capsule person, glowing fake-interior plane or deliberately blurred face substitutes for actual geometry. Separate geometry existence from visual quality and independent acceptance. If an available licensed asset is insufficient, record the gap rather than claim photorealism.
