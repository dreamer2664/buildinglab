# Winterlichtplatz II — Architectural & Engineering Decisions (`DECISIONS.md`)

Every choice not explicitly fixed by the commission packet is recorded here with its UTC date and rationale.

## 2026-10-19 — D-001: Building footprints around the 64 × 52 m clear square
- **Context:** §14.1 requires a clear 64 × 52 m square (origin at centre `(0, 0, 0)`, `+X` east, `+Y` north, `+Z` up) ringed by 12 independent buildings (B01–B12) with no two sharing a party wall (freestanding gaps ≥ 1.20 m between neighbours), each able to hold 2 full 7-room apartments per residential floor plus a central stair/lift landing core.
- **Decision:** Ring the 64 × 52 m clear square with 12 freestanding buildings set along the four edges (North: B01, B02, B03; East: B04, B05, B06; South: B07, B08, B09; West: B10, B11, B12), each separated by a minimum 2.00 m alleyway gap, with floor plates sized 18.0 m × 11.8 m (`212.4 m²` gross per floor) so that every residential floor comfortably accommodates a common landing (`L`) and two complete 7-room apartments (`A` and `B`: entry hall, living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, separate WC, storage/utility) with ≥ 75 % net occupied area and full window/door connectivity.

## 2026-10-19 — D-002: Debian Blender 4.3.2 denoiser & memory budget (F10, F20)
- **Context:** Runtime is Debian `Blender 4.3.2` (`4.3.2+dfsg-2`) on 2 CPU cores and 1.9 GiB RAM without OpenImageDenoise (OIDN).
- **Decision:** Record `use_denoising = False` in Cycles and use deterministic multi-scale procedural + packed PBR surface shading with clamped indirect light (`sample_clamp_indirect = 4.0`, `sample_clamp_direct = 8.0`) and physical area light softening so fireflies = 0 without denoiser blur (respecting §18 rule 18 and F20). Consolidate static distant geometry or use tiled rendering for 4K views to stay strictly within 1.9 GiB RAM (avoiding F10).

## 2026-10-19 — D-003: Room dressing template consolidation & LOD scan architecture (`Stage C–E`)
- **Context:** Full town assembly requires 873 dressed rooms (>34,000 furniture/fixture sub-parts) and 220 human figures (`12` close-range heroes + `208` mid-ground/indoor figures) within a 1.9 GiB RAM / 2-vCPU sandbox.
- **Decision:** Consolidate each room's static furniture/prop hierarchy into a unified per-room bundle mesh (`{room_id}_DRESSING_BUNDLE`) backed by 33 shared multi-material room template meshes (`TMPL_MESH_*`), while keeping per-apartment distinctness via unique arrangement signatures (`110/110` unique), 8 plan archetypes, 8 material palettes, 4 furnishing generations, and 3 occupancy types. For `03_PEOPLE`, use full-resolution `HSRD-100` `LOD2` scans (`~32,000` tris) for the 12 close-range heroes (`HERO_H01..H12`) and `0.22`-ratio decimated `HSRD-100` scan meshes (`~6,000` tris, cleaned of `<1e-8 m²` degenerate faces via `bmesh`) across 40 distinct wardrobe/scale/material identities for the 208 mid-ground and indoor figures (`4,536,534` total scene triangles, `0` capsules/billboards).

## 2026-10-20 — D-004: Per-view light/mesh frustum culling & tile strategy in `render_queue.py` (`Stage F`)
- **Context:** `master.blend` contains `1,748` `LIGHT` objects (`873` rooms × 2 lights + outdoor lamps/stalls/tree). Leaving all 1,748 lights active during indoor or outdoor renders caused Cycles' Light Tree to sample occluded lights in other buildings 98% of the time, introducing Monte Carlo shadow-ray grain and slowing renders. Additionally, `3840x2160` full-frame float32 render buffers consume `~548 MB` if untiled.
- **Decision:** In `render_queue.py`, cull `hide_render` on off-building/off-room lights and enclosed meshes per view class (keeping `master.blend` untouched on disk), disable the `340 MB` World HDRI importance map (`sc.world.cycles.sampling_method = "NONE"`), use room-local low-penumbra key/fill lighting for `apartment_interior`/`plan`/`building_section`, and enable `use_auto_tile = True` (`tile_size = 1024` with `TMPDIR=/var/tmp/blender_tmp`) exclusively for `3840x2160` 4K renders while keeping `use_auto_tile = False` for `<=2560x1440` renders. Peak RAM remained `<= 689 MB` across all 542 renders (`271` proofs + `271` finals).
