## 2. Density Peaks & Queues
1. **Peak 1 — Osler's Crystal Fountain (`(0, 0, 0)`, `B038`):** `220` visitors within `r ≤ 14.0 m` of the octagonal basin, arranged in 3 conversational rings plus families seated on the basin rim.
2. **Peak 2 — The Great Organ & North Transept (`B038` North, `Y ∈ [+18m, +55m]`):** `140` visitors listening beneath the North Transept gallery and seated at the Schweppes Refreshment Pavilion around `ELM_NORTH`.
3. **Peak 3 — The Koh-i-Noor Diamond Gilded Cage (`B039` East Crossing):** `65` visitors queued two-abreast flanked by `4` Royal Sappers & Miners (`S10`) and `2` Metropolitan Police constables (`S11`).
4. **Peak 4 — Machinery in Motion (`B077–B082`):** `260` artisans (`S06`), industrialists (`S03`), and stewards (`S12`) gathered along the iron safety rails of the spinning mules, Jacquard looms, and Applegath vertical printing press.
5. **Peak 5 — Gallery Staircases & Balcony Rails (`Z = 7.32 m`):** `180` visitors ascending the double-flight timber stairs and standing along the gallery balustrades overlooking the Nave and Transept.
6. **Thinning Zones — Raw Materials (`Class I–IV`, `B001–B012`):** `1.2–2.0` visitors per `7.32 m` bay examining coal blocks, ores, and timber sections, providing historically accurate contrast to the crowded Transept and Nave.
