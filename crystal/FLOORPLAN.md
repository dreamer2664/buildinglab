# FLOORPLAN.md — The 24-ft Module Grid, 30 Catalogue Classes & 33 National Courts (PAXTON / CRYSTAL)

Every structural node, exhibit plot, court boundary, gallery stair, and walk station in **CRYSTAL** is indexed to Joseph Paxton's **24-ft (`7.32 m`) module grid** (`G2`, `REF-004`).

## 1. Master Grid Coordinates (`+X = East`, `+Y = North`, `+Z = Up`)

- **Origin `(0.000, 0.000, 0.000)`:** Center of the Crossing (Crystal Fountain pedestal center, ground floor datum).
- **Longitudinal Ranks (`E–W`, `77` bays `B000..B076`, `564.00 m` total length):**
  - **West Nave (`B000..B036`, 37 bays):** `X ∈ [-281.82 m, -10.98 m]` — **United Kingdom & British Colonies** (Western Half of the Palace, per Royal Commission geographic partition).
  - **Central Transept (`B037..B039`, 3 bays = `21.96 m` wide):** `X ∈ [-10.98 m, +10.98 m]`, extending `Y ∈ [-62.22 m, +62.22 m]` (`124.44 m` N–S, crown `Z = 33.00 m`) — **The Crossing, Crystal Fountain, Three Elms, Great Organ, Royal Dais, and Hero Statuary**.
  - **East Nave (`B040..B076`, 37 bays):** `X ∈ [+10.98 m, +281.82 m]` — **Foreign States & Nations** (Eastern Half of the Palace, arranged from the Equator near the Transept outward toward temperate nations).
  - **North Machinery Annex (`B077..B082`, 6 sector blocks, `13.56 m` projection on the North flank):** `X ∈ [-267.18 m, -32.94 m]`, `Y ∈ [+62.22 m, +75.78 m]` — **Machinery in Motion** (steam boilers in the external boiler house feeding live steam via insulated sub-floor pipes).
  - **East & West Terminal Screens & Entrances (`B083..B084`):** `X = ±282.00 m`, South Transept Principal Entrance (`Y = -62.22 m`).
